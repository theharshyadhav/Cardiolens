The CT module is a threshold-based demonstration and the Agatston-style score is simplified and unvalidated. The cardiac-MRI module is an automatic classical image-processing estimate, not validated; the patient's left ventricle is placed in the generic atlas heart by its centre and long axis, which is not an anatomical registration. Nothing on this page is a diagnosis.
